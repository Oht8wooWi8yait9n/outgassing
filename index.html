Database Overview & Spaceflight Screening Criteria

This compilation of outgassing data of materials intended for spacecraft use supersedes NASA Reference Publication 1124, Revision 3. All materials are tested at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center (GSFC) per ASTM E595-77/84/90 (Total Mass Loss (TML) and Collected Volatile Condensable Materials (CVCM) from Outgassing in a Vacuum Environment: 24 hours at 125 °C at <5×10-5 torr, with volatile condensables collected at 25 °C).

  • Total ASTM E595 Material Test Records: 13,582 (`GSFC` / `GSC` Data References, 1967–present)
  • Spaceflight Low-Outgassing Qualified Materials (TML ≤ 1.0% & CVCM ≤ 0.10%): 6,293 (46.3%)
  • Aerospace Manufacturers Indexed: 1,552 Manufacturer 3-Letter Codes (resolved from NASA RP-1124 Appendix A)
